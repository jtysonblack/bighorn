# Bighorn Government Services — Client Profile

## Who They Are

Bighorn Government Services (BGS) is a Service Disabled Veteran Owned Small
Business (SDVOSB) based at 1927 Westland Dr SW, Cleveland, TN 37311. They
offer products and services supporting active duty soldiers and veterans,
with decades of combined experience in munitions, construction, land
management, and information technology across public and private sectors.

## Founder

**Lanny J. Keith** — served in the U.S. Army from 2001 to 2010, left active
duty as a Training NCO. Trained as a 31C Infantry RTO, 11B Infantryman, 19D
Cavalry Scout, Airborne, IED Master Instructor, Harris Satellite
Communications Instructor; attended PSD training with the CIA, FBI, and
Federal Marshals. Received a Meritorious Service Medal and the Bronze Star.
Post-military, worked for Aramid Defense supporting defense contracts —
secured the 2012 General Dynamics Ammunition contract (52 million ammunition
cases and projectiles imported) and the 2014 NEK .308 Match Ammunition
Contract for soldier training. Over the last five years, secured $20M+ in
ammunition component contracts.

## Three Business Lines

### 1. VR Powered Training
Immersive/VR training solutions for healthcare and combat support teams.
**NAICS:** 541511, 334112. Sells on cost-effectiveness, repeatability, and
immersion/retention. Cites Carlton, B. (2017) "VR Learn: Virtual Reality &
Learning" for their retention-rate stat (Lecture 5% / Text 10% / VR 75%).
Four-step rollout methodology: Proof of Concept → Pilot → Content and User
Waves → Organizational Rollout.

### 2. Defense Solutions
Sourcing and distribution of small arms ammunition. **NAICS:** 332992,
332993, 332994. Positions itself as giving government purchasers access to
top-tier manufacturer products meeting the modern warfighter's needs.

### 3. Land Management and Construction
**NAICS:** 237990, 238910, 238990, 561730. Two sub-areas:
- **Land management:** reforestation/plantings, soil containment/erosion
  control, landscape maintenance, GIS/GPS/cartographic services, water
  crossing installation, range/training-area maintenance
  (range-sustainability), road maintenance. Track record: subcontractor on
  the U.S. Army's Land Rehabilitation and Maintenance program (LRAM) for two
  separate prime contractors, across eleven individual U.S. installations
  including Fort Benning, Fort Campbell, and Fort Gordon.
- **Range construction:** 1,000-yard ranges, 2,400-yard UKD ranges, ZAR-
  approved private drop zones, live-action shoot houses, LE qualification
  ranges, MOUT villages, breach/rocket/grenade safety ranges, 360° pistol/
  mobility bays, 200-yard multi-purpose ranges, ARMAG storage areas, onsite
  lodging & retail.
