01 — The Challenge

As healthcare and logistical procedures become more complex in nature, the learning process grows in complexity demanding new pedagogical techniques empowering professionals in developing and maintaining high levels of job proficiency as quickly and effective as possible.

Bighorn Government Service’s immersive and virtual reality powered solution is a one of a kind training tool that addresses ever changing challenges that veteran care and soldier support demands. By training professionals through an immersive environment where multiple methods of learning are employed, individuals are less distracted, learn faster, and retain knowledge at significantly higher rates reducing the impact caused by damage, injury, or death.
