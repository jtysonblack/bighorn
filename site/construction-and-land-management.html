Land Management and Construction Services

01 — Land Management Services

Land Management Services

Bighorn Government Services (BGS) has all the necessary qualifications and experience to manage your property to include:

  • Reforestation & plantings
  • Soil containment and erosion control
  • Landscape maintenance
  • GIS, GPS and other cartographic services
  • Water crossing installation and management
  • Repair and maintenance of ranges and training areas (Range-sustainability)
  • Road maintenance

BGS Team members have successfully completed task orders as a sub on the U.S. Army’s Land Rehabiltation and Maintenance program (LRAM) for two (2) separate prime contractors. BGS has met contractual requirements and directly supported the ITAM coordinators at eleven (11) individual U.S. Installations including Fort Benning, Fort Campbell, Fort Gordon and many others.

BGS personnel have recruited, selected and managed sub-contracted employees on government installations. BGS has the ability to provide training, safety programming, payroll & benefits services, billing and other human resource-related contractual requirements.

BGS can procure PPE, heavy vehicles and other necessary maintenance equipment to the contract as necessary.

BGS also has experience in purchasing and passing through the expense of required ODC’s such as fuel, rock, soil, seed, and landscaping chemicals.

02 — Range Construction Services

Range Construction Services

Bighorn Government Services can design, build and maintain ranges and training facilities. Examples of past projects include:

  • 1,000 yard ranges
  • 2,400 yard UKD ranges
  • Zone Availability Report- listed (ZAR) approved private drop zones (DZ’s)
  • Live action Shoot houses
  • LE qualification ranges
  • MOUT villages
  • Breach facility / rocket and grenade safety ranges
  • 360-degree pistol / mobility bays
  • 200-yard multi-purpose range
  • ARMAG storage areas
  • Onsite lodging & retail
