About Our Founder:

Lanny J. Keith Served in the U.S. Army from 2001 to 2010, leaving active duty as a Training NCO Throughout his thirteen years of service, he was trained as a 31C Infantry RTO, an 11B Infantryman, 19D Cavalry Scout, Airborne, IED Master Instructor, Harris Satellite Communications Instructor, and attended PSD training with the CIA, FBI and Federal Marshals. Lanny has an exemplary service record, receiving a Meritorious Service Medal and the Bronze Star. Following his military career, Lanny began working for Aramid Defense, specializing in the support of Defense contracts. He was successful in securing contracts including the 2012 General Dynamics Ammunition contract to import 52 million ammunition cases and projectiles. In 2014, he was awarded the NEK .308 Match Ammunition Contract for soldier training. Over the last five years, Lanny has secured more than $20 million in ammunition component contracts.
