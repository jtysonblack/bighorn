Our VR Approach

Helping You Adopt a Better Way to Train

We understand that adapting to change and adopting new technology can be risky and disruptive to your current operations. That’s why we’ve developed a multi-step roll-out process to help mitigate your financial risk while ensuring the success of integrating VR powered training into your operational norms.

Our process ensures that you maximize your organization’ s ROI by taking things one step at a time. If you want to learn more about how our methods can help you reduce risk while maximizing your returns, schedule a call with us today.

Rollout Sequence
01

Proof of Concept

We engage with your team to pinpoint targeted areas for process improvement. Working alongside your subject matter experts and our engineers, we will deploy preliminary training modules within a sandbox environment to help your institution gauge the ROI on your VR project.

02

Pilot

After the POC phase, we roll out additional IFU modules for your team(s) based on your institutional priorities. During this phase, additional users will participate in testing the pilot modules for feedback and effectiveness. Based on user feedback, the modules will be shaped and molded to maximize and optimize the delivery of your learning objectives.

03

Content and User Waves

After pilot participants have acclimated to new training environments, modules will be polished both aesthetically and technically to further refine the immersive experience.

04

Organizational Rollout

Once the sand-boxing period has been completed, BGS distributes and integrates with your organization. BGS VR systems then become available to all your employees, managers, and administrators for use. We also assist with building out your simulation space, as we provide a total end-to-end solution. You have nothing to worry about. Focus on your learning objectives and we will take care of the rest.
